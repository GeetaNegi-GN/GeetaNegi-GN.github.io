# Privacy Policy — The Absolute Void

**Effective date:** October 25, 2026  
**App name:** The Absolute Void

## 1. Introduction

The Absolute Void (“the App”) is a minimalist focus and distraction-reduction application designed to help users spend time in quiet, uninterrupted focus.

Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used, where it is stored, and what choices you have.

The App is designed around a privacy-first approach, with core functionality intended to work locally on your device without requiring an account.

## 2. Information We Collect

The App is intended to operate without collecting personally identifiable information.

Depending on the features you use, the App may store the following information locally on your device:

- **Focus sessions:** Session start times, durations, pause and resume states, and completion status.
- **App preferences:** Selected ambient color, session duration preferences, and disturbance-warning settings.
- **Focus streaks:** Information used to calculate consecutive days of completed focus sessions.
- **Session history:** If this feature is available and enabled, records of completed or cancelled sessions.

This information is used to operate the App, restore active sessions, remember your preferences, and display relevant focus statistics.

The App does not require you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or create an account to use its core features.

## 3. How Your Information Is Used

Locally stored information may be used to:

- Start, pause, resume, and end focus sessions.
- Restore session state when the App is reopened.
- Remember your preferences between uses.
- Calculate focus streaks and display session history, where supported.
- Provide optional reminders or completion notifications, if enabled.

The App is designed to use this information only to provide its functionality.

## 4. Data Storage and Sharing

The App is intended to store its core settings and focus-session information locally on your device.

We do not sell your personal information. The App is not designed to transmit your focus-session records to an external server.

We do not intentionally share locally stored focus-session information with advertisers or other third parties.

Please note that Android device backups, if enabled on your device and supported by the App's backup configuration, may store app data through your chosen backup service. The handling of such backups is also subject to the relevant service provider's policies.

## 5. Permissions

The App may use Android system capabilities necessary for its features. Depending on your device and the features enabled, these may include:

- **Notifications:** To display optional focus-session completion notifications or other user-enabled notifications.
- **Vibration:** To provide a subtle haptic response when a session ends.
- **Device motion sensors:** To detect a shake gesture for disturbance warnings, if supported and enabled.
- **Scheduling capabilities:** To manage session timing or scheduled focus sessions, if supported by the installed version.

Permissions are used only for their intended app functionality. The availability and behavior of certain features may vary by Android version and device settings.

## 6. Third-Party Services

The App is intended to provide its core focus experience without requiring third-party accounts or external services.

If a future version integrates analytics, advertising, crash reporting, cloud storage, payment processing, or other third-party services, those services may process certain information according to their own privacy policies. This policy will be updated as necessary to describe those integrations.

Google Play and Android may independently process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored by the App generally remains on your device until it is deleted or the relevant app data is cleared.

Where session history deletion is available, you can use that feature to delete your stored history.

You can also clear the App's storage or uninstall the App through Android settings. These actions may delete locally stored settings, session records, streak information, and other app data.

Clearing app data or uninstalling the App may prevent previously stored session information from being recovered. Data included in device backups may be subject to separate retention and deletion procedures.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the core experience does not require an account or personal details, users can access its intended functionality without submitting such information.

If you believe that personal information has been collected through the App inappropriately, please contact us using the details below.

## 9. Security

We aim to minimize privacy risks by keeping the core experience simple and limiting the information the App needs to function.

However, no method of electronic storage or software security can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should protect access to your device and keep its operating system up to date.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

Any revised version will be published at the location where this policy is made available. The effective date at the top will indicate when the latest version took effect.
